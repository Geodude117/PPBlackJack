Overview

Perfect Pair Blackjack at ppblackjack.co.uk ("the Site") is run by Geovan Inacay ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what data is used when you play the game in your browser, and how it is handled.

The Site is a free, play-money game. There is no real-money gambling, no prizes and nothing to buy.

Data we collect

Stored in your browser only

  • Your chip balance
  • Your game settings (sound, strategy hints, deal speed, animations, shoe size)

These are saved using your browser's local storage so the game remembers them next time. They stay on your device and are never sent to us. You can clear them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

We do not collect

  • Your name, email address or any account information. There are no accounts.
  • Your precise location
  • Anything that identifies you personally. Visitor statistics are anonymous (see below).

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and understand how the Site is used, for example how many people visit, which pages they view, which website or search sent them, the type of device and browser, and the country they're in.

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies, does not store anything on your device, and does not track you across other websites or build a profile of you. The figures we see are totals only. We cannot identify individual visitors. See Cloudflare Web Analytics and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Advertising and cookies

  • show ads, including ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites (personalised ads)
  • measure how ads perform
  • prevent fraud and abuse

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads or advertising cookies are used. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change your choice at any time using the "Privacy and cookie settings" link that Google's consent message adds to the page.

You can also manage personalised advertising from Google at adssettings.google.com. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Other services the Site relies on

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal data, including the right to access it, correct it, delete it and object to its use. Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves (our visitor statistics are anonymous totals), most requests about advertising data are best directed to Google through the links above. You are also welcome to contact us. If you are unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
