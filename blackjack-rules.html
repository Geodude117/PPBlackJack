How to Play Blackjack

Blackjack is a card game you play against the dealer. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over, and you win. Here are the full rules, as played in Perfect Pair Blackjack.

How a round works

  1. Bet. Place your main bet. You can add the optional side bets at the same time.
  2. Deal. You get two cards face up. The dealer gets one face up (the up card) and one face down (the hole card).
  3. Dealer checks for blackjack. If the dealer shows an Ace you’re offered insurance. If the dealer has blackjack, the round ends straight away.
  4. Your turn. Hit, stand, double down or split until you stand or go over 21.
  5. Dealer’s turn. The dealer turns over the hole card and draws until reaching 17 or more.
  6. Settle. Higher total wins. Going over 21 (a bust) loses straight away, even if the dealer busts afterwards.

Soft and hard hands

A soft hand has an Ace counting as 11, such as A,6 (soft 17). It can’t bust on the next card, because the Ace can drop to 1. A hard hand either has no Ace or has an Ace that must count as 1, such as 10,6,A (hard 17).

The difference matters: you’d always stand on hard 17, but basic strategy says to hit or double a soft 17.

Blackjack rules FAQ

What is the aim of blackjack?

To finish with a hand closer to 21 than the dealer’s without going over. You are only playing against the dealer, not the other players.

What is the difference between blackjack and 21?

A blackjack is exactly two cards, an Ace and a 10-value card, and pays 3:2. Any other hand that totals 21, such as 7, 7 and 7, is just 21 and pays 1:1.

What happens if you tie with the dealer?

It is a push. Your bet is returned and you neither win nor lose.

Does the dealer have to hit on 16?

Yes. The dealer has no choices: they must hit on 16 or less and stand on 17 or more. In Perfect Pair Blackjack the dealer also hits a soft 17.
