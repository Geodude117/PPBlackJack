How to play blackjack

  1. Place your bet. Pick a chip (1, 5, 25 or 50) and tap the BET circle, or drag the chip onto it. You can add the optional PP and 21+3 side bets too.
  2. Deal. You and the dealer get two cards each. Your cards are face up; the dealer shows one card and keeps one face down.
  3. Make your move. Get as close to 21 as you can without going over. Hit takes another card, Stand keeps your hand, Double doubles your bet for exactly one more card, and Split turns a pair into two separate hands.
  4. Dealer's turn. The dealer reveals the hidden card and draws until reaching 17 or more, hitting soft 17. Beat the dealer's total without busting to win.

Number cards count at face value, J, Q and K count as 10, and an Ace counts as 1 or 11. A blackjack (an Ace with a 10-value card as your first two cards) pays 3:2. A normal win pays 1:1, and a tie is a push, so your bet comes back.

Blackjack side bets explained

With a six-deck shoe, about 7% of starting hands are a pair of some kind, and about 1.6% (roughly 1 in 60) are a perfect pair of identical cards. That's why a perfect pair pays 30:1.
